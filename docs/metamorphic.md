A **relation** changes one thing in a design, keeps its loads, and says how every check must answer. A **monotone** relation makes a part thicker, adds a bolt or raises a strength, so no check's utilisation may rise. An **invariant** relation restates the design in other units, so every check must read exactly the same. The **mirror** reflects the bolt group about an uncoped beam's mid-depth. A positional check swaps top for bottom and keeps its outcome, and every other check keeps its numbers.

Each relation is read per check, never per verdict. A check whose clause lets it move the wrong way is in the exemption table below, with that clause. A PASS that a monotone relation turns into a FAIL must be carried by a check that broke the relation or by an exempt check above 1. An invariant relation and the mirror must also keep the verdict and the modes that fail. A design whose card gives a length as a range is read over its box by a search that stops within 1/2000 of a point, so a monotone relation or the mirror allows it a relative slack of 1/1000. Other designs get 1e-9, and an invariant relation gets none.
